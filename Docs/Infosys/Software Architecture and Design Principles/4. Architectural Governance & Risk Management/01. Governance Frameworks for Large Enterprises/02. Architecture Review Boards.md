# Architecture Review Boards (ARBs)
## 1. What is it?
- A formal governance body within an enterprise that oversees architectural decisions across projects and systems.  
- Core Infosys concept: ARBs ensure quality, consistency, compliance, and alignment with business goals and standards.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Strategic Alignment → ensures architecture supports organizational objectives.  
- Standardization → enforces enterprise principles and guidelines.  
- Risk Management → identifies and mitigates risks early.  
- Quality Assurance → validates scalability, security, and performance.  
- Change Control → reviews and approves major architectural changes.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Responsibilities: review proposals, approve standards, monitor compliance, facilitate communication, provide guidance.  
- Composition: enterprise architects, solution architects, domain experts, business stakeholders, governance officers.  
- Situational traps: ARBs perceived as bureaucratic or slowing projects.  
- Keywords: compliance, risk, quality, governance, alignment.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Large enterprise implementing ARB for cloud migration:  
  - Problem → inconsistent designs across business units.  
  - ARB → reviewed proposals, enforced standards, ensured compliance.  
  - Outcome → unified architecture, reduced risks, improved scalability.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: What role does an ARB play in enterprise governance?  
  A: It acts as a decision‑making authority ensuring architectural consistency, compliance, and alignment with business goals.  
- Q: How would you address the perception of ARBs as bureaucratic?  
  A: Streamline processes, focus on enabling rather than restricting, and communicate value to stakeholders.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating ARBs as restrictive instead of enabling.  
- Overloading ARBs with too many reviews, slowing down delivery.  
- Lack of skilled professionals in ARB composition.  
- Poor communication between ARB and project teams.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Quality Gatekeepers”** — ARBs are checkpoints ensuring every architectural decision meets standards before moving forward.  
- Mnemonic: **“SRQMC → Strategic alignment, Risk, Quality, Monitoring, Change control.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- ARB = governance body for architecture decisions.  
- Purpose → alignment, standardization, risk management, quality assurance, change control.  
- Responsibilities → review, approve, monitor, guide.  
- Composition → architects, domain experts, business stakeholders, governance officers.  
- Exam trap → ARBs seen as bureaucratic instead of enablers.
