# Governance Framework
## 1. What is it?
- A structured set of principles, processes, roles, and tools guiding decision‑making and accountability in an organization.  
- Core Infosys concept: ensures IT systems, business processes, and strategic initiatives align with corporate objectives while maintaining compliance, security, and efficiency.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Complexity Management → large enterprises operate across geographies, units, and technologies.  
- Risk Mitigation → manages risks in security, compliance, and technology adoption.  
- Regulatory Compliance → ensures adherence to laws and standards.  
- Strategic Alignment → aligns IT investments and architecture with business goals.  
- Transparency & Accountability → establishes clear roles and responsibilities.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Components: policies, roles, processes, metrics, tools.  
- Frameworks: COBIT, TOGAF, ITIL, ISO 38500.  
- Domains: IT, data, security, architecture, project/portfolio governance.  
- Situational traps: treating governance as bureaucracy instead of enabler.  
- Keywords: compliance, accountability, risk, alignment.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Global enterprise adopting TOGAF governance:  
  - Problem → fragmented architecture decisions across regions.  
  - Framework → TOGAF governance boards + KPIs.  
  - Outcome → standardized processes, reduced duplication, improved compliance.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: Why are governance frameworks critical in large enterprises?  
  A: They manage complexity, mitigate risks, ensure compliance, and align IT with business strategy.  
- Q: How would you differentiate COBIT and TOGAF in governance?  
  A: COBIT focuses on IT governance and management; TOGAF provides structured enterprise architecture governance.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Viewing governance as bureaucratic overhead.  
- Ignoring cultural resistance and change management.  
- Failing to integrate governance with existing systems.  
- Neglecting continuous monitoring and KPIs.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Traffic Rules for Enterprises”** — governance frameworks are the rules ensuring safe, efficient, and aligned movement across organizational roads.  
- Mnemonic: **“PRPMT → Policies, Roles, Processes, Metrics, Tools.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Governance framework = structured principles/processes for accountability.

- Frameworks → COBIT, TOGAF, ITIL, ISO.  
- Domains → IT, data, security, architecture, portfolio.
