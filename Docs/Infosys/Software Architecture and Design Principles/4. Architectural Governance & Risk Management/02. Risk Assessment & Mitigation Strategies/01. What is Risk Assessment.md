# Risk Assessment
## 1. What is it?
- A systematic process of identifying, analyzing, and evaluating potential risks that could negatively impact organizational objectives, operations, or assets.  
- Core Infosys concept: risk assessment prioritizes actions to minimize exposure and strengthen resilience.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Proactive Risk Management → detect issues before escalation.  
- Compliance → meet regulatory and industry standards.  
- Business Continuity → ensure resilience during disruptions.  
- Cost Efficiency → avoid financial losses from unmanaged risks.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Steps: identification, analysis, evaluation.  
- Categories: operational, financial, compliance, technology, strategic.  
- Mitigation strategies: avoidance, reduction, transfer, acceptance.  
- Tools: risk matrices, FMEA, scenario analysis, heat maps.  
- Situational traps: ignoring dynamic risks (AI misuse, cloud vulnerabilities).  
- Keywords: likelihood, impact, risk appetite, mitigation.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Enterprise cloud migration risk assessment:  
  - Risks → cybersecurity threats, compliance gaps, downtime.  
  - Analysis → likelihood vs. impact using heat maps.  
  - Mitigation → redundancy, encryption, compliance audits.  
  - Outcome → secure, compliant, resilient cloud adoption.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: What are the key steps in risk assessment?  
  A: Identify risks, analyze likelihood/impact, evaluate against risk appetite, prioritize mitigation.  
- Q: How does risk assessment integrate with governance frameworks?  
  A: It aligns with COBIT, ISO 31000, ARBs, and compliance programs to validate technology risks.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating risk assessment as one‑time instead of continuous.  
- Ignoring emerging risks (AI misuse, cloud vulnerabilities).  
- Underestimating resource constraints for risk programs.  
- Cultural resistance → lack of awareness or stakeholder buy‑in.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Enterprise Radar System”** — risk assessment scans the environment to detect threats before they hit.  
- Mnemonic: **“IAE → Identify, Analyze, Evaluate.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Risk assessment = systematic identification, analysis, evaluation of risks.  
- Categories → operational, financial, compliance, technology, strategic.  
- Mitigation → avoidance, reduction, transfer, acceptance.  
- Tools → matrices, FMEA, scenario analysis, heat maps.  
- Exam trap → ignoring dynamic risks or treating assessment as one‑time.
