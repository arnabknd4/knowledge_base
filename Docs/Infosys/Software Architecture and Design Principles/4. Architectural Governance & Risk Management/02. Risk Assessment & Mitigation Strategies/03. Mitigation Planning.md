# Mitigation Planning
## 1. What is it?
- The process of developing strategies and actions to reduce the likelihood or impact of identified risks.  
- Core Infosys concept: proactive risk management embedded into enterprise governance to strengthen resilience.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Risk Reduction → minimize potential damage from threats.  
- Business Continuity → maintain operations during disruptions.  
- Compliance → meet regulatory and industry standards.  
- Cost Efficiency → avoid financial losses and reputational harm.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Steps: prioritization, objectives, strategies, responsibilities, action plans, implementation, monitoring.  
- Strategies: avoidance, reduction, transfer, acceptance.  
- Tools: risk registers, contingency plans, BIA, scenario planning.  
- Situational traps: ignoring resource constraints or stakeholder buy‑in.  
- Keywords: resilience, KPIs, governance, continuity.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Enterprise data center risk mitigation:  
  - Risk → downtime due to power failure.  
  - Strategy → redundancy + backup generators.  
  - Action plan → assign infra team, monitor uptime KPIs.  
  - Outcome → improved resilience, reduced downtime, compliance confidence.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: What are the key strategies in mitigation planning?  
  A: Avoidance, reduction, transfer, acceptance — chosen based on risk appetite and feasibility.  
- Q: How does mitigation planning support business continuity?  
  A: By proactively reducing vulnerabilities and ensuring contingency plans are in place for disruptions.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating mitigation as one‑time instead of continuous.  
- Ignoring dynamic risks (cyberattacks, AI misuse).  
- Underestimating resource constraints.  
- Lack of stakeholder buy‑in due to perceived cost.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Enterprise Safety Net”** — mitigation planning catches risks before they cause major damage.  
- Mnemonic: **“PRAMOSI → Prioritize, Objectives, Mitigation, Assign, Action, Monitor.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Mitigation planning = proactive strategies to reduce risk likelihood/impact.  
- Steps → prioritize → set objectives → strategies → assign → action plan → monitor.
