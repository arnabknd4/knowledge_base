# Sustainability in Enterprise Architecture
## 1. What is it?
- Designing and managing IT systems and infrastructure to minimize environmental impact, optimize resource usage, and support long‑term ecological balance.  
- Core Infosys concept: integrating green practices into technology decisions so growth aligns with ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) goals.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Environmental Responsibility → reduces carbon footprint and energy consumption.  
- Regulatory Compliance → meets global sustainability standards and laws.  
- Cost Efficiency → lowers operational costs through energy optimization.  
- Brand Reputation → demonstrates CSR and attracts eco‑conscious stakeholders.  
- Future Readiness → aligns with global green IT and ESG trends.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: energy efficiency, resource optimization, lifecycle management, renewable integration, carbon footprint reduction.  
- Strategies: cloud adoption, virtualization, green data centers, paperless processes, monitoring tools.  
- Situational traps: ignoring upfront investment or measurement complexity.  
- Keywords: ESG, carbon neutrality, virtualization, green IT.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Global enterprise adopting green data centers:  
  - Strategy → advanced cooling + renewable energy.  
  - Outcome → reduced energy bills, compliance with sustainability regulations, improved brand reputation.  
- Another case → virtualization to consolidate servers, reducing hardware footprint and e‑waste.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: Why is sustainability important in enterprise architecture?  
  A: It reduces environmental impact, ensures compliance, lowers costs, and enhances brand reputation.  
- Q: How can enterprises implement sustainability strategies?  
  A: Through cloud adoption, virtualization, renewable energy integration, and monitoring carbon emissions.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating sustainability as optional instead of strategic.  
- Ignoring cultural resistance and stakeholder buy‑in.  
- Underestimating upfront investment in green technologies.  
- Difficulty in quantifying sustainability impact due to poor metrics.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Green Blueprint”** — sustainability ensures enterprise architecture builds responsibly for both business and planet.  
- Mnemonic: **“ERCCF → Energy, Resource, Cycle, Carbon, Footprint.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
