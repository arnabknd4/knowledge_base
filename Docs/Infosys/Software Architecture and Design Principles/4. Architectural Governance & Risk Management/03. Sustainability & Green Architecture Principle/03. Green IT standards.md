# Green IT Standards
## 1. What is it?
- Guidelines, frameworks, and best practices ensuring IT systems and processes are environmentally sustainable.  
- Core Infosys concept: reduce energy consumption, minimize carbon footprint, and promote responsible resource usage across the IT lifecycle.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Environmental Sustainability → reduce ecological impact of IT operations.  
- Regulatory Compliance → meet global environmental laws and certifications.  
- Cost Optimization → lower energy and operational costs.  
- CSR → demonstrate corporate responsibility and attract eco‑conscious stakeholders.  
- Risk Reduction → avoid penalties and reputational damage.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Standards: ISO 14001, ISO 50001, Energy Star, EPEAT, LEED, EU Eco‑Design Directive.  
- Principles: energy efficiency, lifecycle management, sustainable procurement, carbon footprint reduction, waste management.  
- Situational traps: ignoring upfront investment or measurement complexity.  
- Keywords: eco‑certification, recyclability, carbon neutrality, green procurement.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Enterprise adopting ISO 50001 for energy management:  
  - Strategy → monitor and optimize energy usage across data centers.  
  - Outcome → reduced consumption, compliance with global standards, cost savings.  
- Another case → EPEAT‑certified laptops procured for employees, reducing e‑waste and improving sustainability scores.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: What are the key Green IT standards enterprises should adopt?  
  A: ISO 14001 (environmental management), ISO 50001 (energy management), Energy Star (hardware efficiency), EPEAT (product recyclability), LEED (green buildings).  
- Q: How do Green IT standards support enterprise sustainability goals?  
  A: They enforce energy efficiency, lifecycle management, and compliance, aligning IT with ESG objectives.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating standards as optional instead of mandatory compliance.  
- Ignoring integration complexity with existing IT frameworks.  
- Underestimating upfront investment in green technologies.  
- Difficulty in quantifying sustainability impact due to poor metrics.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Green Rulebook”** — standards act as the playbook ensuring IT runs sustainably.  
- Mnemonic: **“IELEN → ISO 14001, Energy Star, LEED, EPEAT, ISO 50001, EU Directive.”**
