# EPEAT – Electronic Product Environmental Assessment Tool
## 1. What is it?
- A global eco‑rating system that evaluates IT products (computers, monitors, servers, etc.) for environmental impact and recyclability.  
- Core Infosys concept: ensures enterprises procure hardware that meets sustainability criteria across the product lifecycle.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Environmental Sustainability → reduces e‑waste and promotes recyclability.  
- Compliance → aligns with international procurement standards.  
- Cost Efficiency → lowers lifecycle costs through durable, eco‑certified products.  
- CSR → demonstrates commitment to sustainable procurement.  
- Risk Reduction → avoids reputational damage from unsustainable sourcing.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Scope: laptops, desktops, monitors, servers, imaging equipment.  
- Principles: lifecycle management, recyclability, sustainable procurement.  
- Situational traps: assuming all vendors comply without certification.  
- Keywords: eco‑certification, recyclability, lifecycle, procurement.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Enterprise procurement strategy:  
  - Action → purchase EPEAT‑certified laptops and monitors.  
  - Outcome → reduced e‑waste, compliance with sustainability goals, improved ESG scores.  
- Another case → government agencies mandating EPEAT‑certified IT products for procurement.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: What is the purpose of EPEAT certification?  
  A: To evaluate IT products for environmental impact and recyclability, ensuring sustainable procurement.  
- Q: How does EPEAT differ from Energy Star?  
  A: EPEAT focuses on lifecycle sustainability and recyclability, while Energy Star emphasizes energy efficiency.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating EPEAT as optional instead of mandatory for procurement.  
- Ignoring lifecycle management beyond purchase.  
- Assuming certification alone guarantees sustainability without proper disposal practices.  
- Overlooking employee awareness of eco‑certified usage.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Eco Report Card”** — EPEAT grades IT products on sustainability performance.  
- Mnemonic: **“LRS → Lifecycle, Recyclability, Sustainable procurement.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- EPEAT = eco‑rating system for IT products.  
- Benefits → sustainability, compliance, cost efficiency, CSR.  
- Scope → laptops, desktops, monitors, servers, imaging equipment.  
- Exam trap → assuming compliance without certification or ignoring lifecycle impact.
