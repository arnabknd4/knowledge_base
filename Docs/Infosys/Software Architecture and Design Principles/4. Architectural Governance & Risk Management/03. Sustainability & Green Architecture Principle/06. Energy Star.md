# Energy Star Certification
## 1. What is it?
- A globally recognized certification program for energy‑efficient hardware and devices.  
- Core Infosys concept: ensures IT equipment meets strict energy efficiency standards, reducing power consumption and environmental impact.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Environmental Sustainability → lowers ecological footprint of IT operations.  
- Cost Savings → reduces electricity bills and operational expenses.  
- Compliance → aligns with international energy efficiency regulations.  
- CSR → demonstrates commitment to green IT practices.  
- Risk Reduction → avoids penalties and reputational damage from inefficient systems.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Scope: desktops, laptops, servers, monitors, printers, data center equipment.  
- Principles: energy efficiency, reduced idle power usage, optimized performance.  
- Situational traps: assuming all devices are compliant without certification.  
- Keywords: energy efficiency, eco‑label, certified hardware.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Enterprise procurement strategy:  
  - Action → purchase Energy Star‑certified laptops and servers.  
  - Outcome → reduced energy bills, compliance with sustainability goals, improved ESG scores.  
- Another case → data centers adopting Energy Star‑rated cooling systems for efficiency.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: What is the purpose of Energy Star certification in IT?  
  A: To ensure devices meet energy efficiency standards, reducing costs and environmental impact.  
- Q: How does Energy Star differ from ISO standards?  
  A: Energy Star focuses on product‑level efficiency certification, while ISO standards provide organizational frameworks for sustainability.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating Energy Star as optional instead of mandatory for procurement.  
- Assuming certification alone guarantees sustainability without lifecycle management.  
- Ignoring non‑certified devices in hybrid environments.  
- Overlooking employee awareness of energy‑efficient usage.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Eco Seal of Approval”** — Energy Star is like a badge proving devices consume less energy.  
- Mnemonic: **“SAVE → Sustainability, Approval, Verified Efficiency.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Energy Star = certification for energy‑efficient IT hardware.  
- Benefits → sustainability, compliance, cost savings, CSR.  
- Scope → laptops, servers, monitors, printers, cooling systems.  
- Exam trap → assuming compliance without certification or ignoring lifecycle impact.
