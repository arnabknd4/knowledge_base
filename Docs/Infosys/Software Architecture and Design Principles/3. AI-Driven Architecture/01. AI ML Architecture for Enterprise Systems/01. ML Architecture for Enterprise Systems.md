# AI/ML Architecture for Enterprise Systems
## 1. What is it?
- The design and integration of AI/ML capabilities into enterprise IT environments.  
- Core Infosys concept: embedding intelligence into systems to enable predictive analytics, automation, and adaptive decision‑making.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Enhances business agility with faster, data‑driven decisions.  
- Improves operational efficiency by automating repetitive tasks.  
- Provides actionable insights from raw data.  
- Creates competitive advantage through innovation and personalization.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: scalability, security, governance, integration.  
- Situational traps: ignoring data quality or legacy integration challenges.  
- Keywords: predictive analytics, automation, personalization, governance, scalability.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Retail enterprise adopting AI/ML:  
  - Goal → personalized shopping experiences.  
  - Architecture → ML recommendation engine integrated with CRM and ERP.  
  - Outcome → improved customer engagement, optimized inventory, reduced costs.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: Why is AI/ML architecture critical for enterprises?  
  A: It enables agility, efficiency, and innovation by embedding intelligence into workflows and customer experiences.  
- Q: What challenges must be addressed in AI/ML adoption?  
  A: Data quality, legacy system integration, governance/ethics, and scalability.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Deploying AI without addressing data quality issues.  
- Treating AI/ML as isolated pilots instead of enterprise‑wide architecture.  
- Ignoring governance, fairness, and compliance.  
- Underestimating scalability requirements for global deployments.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Brain for the Enterprise”** — AI/ML acts as the brain, processing data and guiding decisions across systems.  
- Mnemonic: **“PACS → Predictive, Automation, Customer personalization, Scalability.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- AI/ML architecture = embedding intelligence into enterprise systems.  
- Benefits → predictive analytics, automation, personalization, cost optimization.  
- Challenges → data quality, integration complexity, governance, scalability.  
- Exam trap → focusing only on models without enterprise integration.
