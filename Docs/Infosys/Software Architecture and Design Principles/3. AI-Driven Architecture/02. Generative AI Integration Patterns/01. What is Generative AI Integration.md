# Generative AI Integration
## 1. What is it?
- Generative AI = AI models that create new content (text, images, audio, code, synthetic data) based on learned patterns.  
- Integration = embedding GenAI into enterprise systems using architectural patterns that ensure scalability, security, performance, and business alignment.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Standardization → repeatable methods for connecting GenAI to workflows.  
- Risk Mitigation → compliance, security, and ethical AI use.  
- Cost Efficiency → optimized infrastructure, reduced duplication.  
- Business Alignment → measurable outcomes tied to enterprise goals.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: scalability, security, compliance, performance optimization, alignment.  
- Situational traps: deploying GenAI pilots without integration patterns.  
- Keywords: APIs, orchestration, governance, ROI, ethical AI.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Enterprise customer support automation:  
  - Capability → AI‑generated responses for FAQs.  
  - Integration → API‑driven GenAI connected to CRM.  
  - Outcome → reduced response time, improved customer satisfaction, measurable ROI.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: Why are integration patterns critical for GenAI adoption?  
  A: They provide standardization, mitigate risks, optimize costs, and ensure business alignment.  
- Q: How would you integrate GenAI into enterprise workflows?  
  A: Use APIs for modular integration, enforce governance for compliance, and align outputs with KPIs.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating GenAI as isolated pilots without enterprise integration.  
- Ignoring compliance and ethical considerations.  
- Overlooking scalability for large workloads.  
- Failing to link GenAI outputs to measurable business outcomes.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“GenAI as a New Power Source”** — integration patterns are the wiring that safely connects it to enterprise systems.  
- Mnemonic: **“SCRB → Scalability, Compliance, Risk, Business alignment.”**
