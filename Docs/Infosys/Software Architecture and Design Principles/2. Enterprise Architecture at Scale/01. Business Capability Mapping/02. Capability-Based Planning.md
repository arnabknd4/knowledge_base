# Capability-Based Planning (CBP)
## 1. What is it?
- A strategic planning approach that prioritizes initiatives based on the capabilities needed to achieve business objectives.  
- Core Infosys concept: start with capabilities, not projects or technologies.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Aligns IT investments with business strategy.  
- Improves prioritization by focusing on critical capabilities.  
- Provides resilience since capabilities remain stable even when processes or technologies change.  
- Enables transformation by evolving capabilities to meet future demands.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: strategy first, capability identification, maturity assessment, gap prioritization.  
- Situational traps: starting with projects or tech trends instead of capabilities.  
- Keywords: capability maturity, prioritization, alignment, transformation roadmap.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Retail company aiming to improve customer experience:  
  - Strategic goal → personalization + faster delivery.  
  - Required capabilities → Customer Analytics + Order Fulfillment.  
  - Initiatives → advanced analytics tools + upgraded warehouse systems.  
  - Outcome → improved personalization and reduced delivery time.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How does CBP differ from traditional project‑based planning?  
  A: CBP starts with capabilities (what the business must do) and links initiatives to strategic goals, while project‑based planning often begins with technology or organizational structures.  
- Q: How would you assess capability maturity in CBP?  
  A: Use maturity models to evaluate current vs. desired performance, identify gaps, and prioritize initiatives accordingly.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating capabilities as processes or organizational units.  
- Ignoring maturity assessment, leading to misaligned investments.  
- Prioritizing technology trends without business linkage.  
- Failing to link initiatives to measurable outcomes.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Capability Lens”** — CBP is like putting on glasses that focus on what the business must do, not how or with which tools.  
- Mnemonic: **“SIPGP → Strategy, Identify, Prioritize, Gaps, Plan.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- CBP = plan based on capabilities, not projects.  
- Aligns IT with business strategy.  
- Capabilities are stable, business‑focused, tech‑agnostic.  
- Assess maturity → prioritize gaps → plan initiatives.  
- Example: analytics + fulfillment for retail personalization.  
- Exam trap: starting with technology instead of capabilities.
