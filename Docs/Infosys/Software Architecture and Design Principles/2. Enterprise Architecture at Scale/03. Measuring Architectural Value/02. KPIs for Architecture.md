# KPIs for Architecture
## 1. What is it?
- KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) are measurable values that track how effectively enterprise architecture (EA) delivers business value.  
- Core Infosys concept: KPIs quantify architecture’s impact on agility, cost, risk, compliance, and innovation.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Provides visibility into architecture’s contribution to business outcomes.  
- Ensures accountability by linking EA to ROI.  
- Supports decision‑making and prioritization of initiatives.  
- Enables continuous improvement by identifying gaps and optimization areas.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Categories: Cost Optimization, Business Agility, Standardization & Compliance, Risk Reduction, Business Alignment, Innovation Enablement.  
- Situational traps: focusing only on technical metrics instead of business outcomes.  
- Keywords: NPS, ROI, compliance, agility, innovation adoption.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Infosys client consolidating applications:  
  - KPI → Annual cost savings.  
  - Outcome → Reduced maintenance costs, improved efficiency.  
- Another case → API‑driven architecture:  
  - KPI → Time‑to‑market reduction.  
  - Outcome → Faster partner integration, improved customer satisfaction.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How would you select KPIs for architecture initiatives?  
  A: Align KPIs with business objectives, choose relevant metrics (cost, agility, compliance, innovation), set targets, and monitor via dashboards.  
- Q: Why are KPIs critical for EA governance?  
  A: They demonstrate measurable value, build stakeholder confidence, and enable prioritization.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Selecting KPIs without linkage to strategic goals.  
- Overloading dashboards with irrelevant technical metrics.  
- Treating KPI measurement as one‑time instead of iterative.  
- Ignoring stakeholder satisfaction and alignment metrics.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Architecture Scorecard”** — KPIs are the score that shows how well EA plays the business game.  
- Mnemonic: **“CABRBI → Cost, Agility, Business alignment, Risk, Business compliance, Innovation.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- KPIs = measurable link between EA and business outcomes.  
- Categories → cost optimization, agility, compliance, risk, alignment, innovation.  
- Implementation → define objectives, select KPIs, set targets, monitor/report, review/improve.  
- Exam trap → focusing only on technical metrics or ignoring business linkage.
