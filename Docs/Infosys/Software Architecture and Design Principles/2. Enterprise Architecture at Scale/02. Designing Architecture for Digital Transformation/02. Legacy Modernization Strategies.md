# Legacy Modernization Strategies
## 1. What is it?
- The process of upgrading, transforming, or replacing outdated IT systems to meet current business needs and support digital transformation.  
- Core Infosys concept: modernization ensures agility, scalability, integration, and compliance.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Legacy systems are costly, insecure, and slow down innovation.  
- Modernization reduces operational costs, improves agility, and enables integration with cloud, APIs, and emerging technologies.  
- Ensures compliance with modern security and regulatory standards.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Strategies: Rehosting, Replatforming, Refactoring, Rearchitecting, Rebuilding, Replacing.  
- Situational traps: choosing “lift and shift” without addressing architectural limitations.  
- Keywords: agility, scalability, compliance, cost reduction.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Banking client with 20‑year‑old core system:  
  - Problem → high maintenance cost, poor mobile integration.  
  - Strategy → rearchitect into microservices + migrate to cloud.  
  - Outcome → improved agility, scalability, and customer experience.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How would you decide which modernization strategy to use?  
  A: Assess business impact, cost vs. value, risk, and future needs. Choose strategy balancing agility, scalability, and ROI.  
- Q: What’s the difference between rehosting and rearchitecting?  
  A: Rehosting = lift‑and‑shift with minimal change; Rearchitecting = redesign for modern architecture (microservices, APIs).

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating rehosting as a long‑term solution.  
- Ignoring compliance/security risks in legacy systems.  
- Overlooking dependencies and integration needs.  
- Choosing rebuilding/replacing without considering business process changes.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“House Renovation Spectrum”** — from repainting (rehosting) to rebuilding from scratch (rebuilding).  
- Mnemonic: **“6R → Rehost, Replatform, Refactor, Rearchitect, Rebuild, Replace.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Legacy modernization = upgrade/replace outdated systems.  
- Strategies → 6R model.  
- Key drivers → agility, cost reduction, compliance, integration.  
- Example → bank rearchitecting core system for cloud + microservices.  
- Exam trap → assuming lift‑and‑shift solves modernization challenges.
