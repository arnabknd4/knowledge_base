# Roadmap Creation Techniques
## 1. What is it?
- A systematic approach to convert high‑level vision into actionable, phased initiatives.  
- Core Infosys concept: techniques ensure clarity, alignment, and feasibility in enterprise planning.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Prevents abstract visions from remaining unexecuted.  
- Links business capabilities with technology enablers.  
- Provides realistic timelines, dependency sequencing, and stakeholder clarity.  
- Strengthens governance and reduces delivery risks.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: decomposition, capability mapping, phased planning, dependency analysis, visualization.  
- Situational traps: ignoring dependencies or treating roadmaps as static timelines.  
- Keywords: vision decomposition, capability mapping, time‑phased, dependencies, visualization.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Infosys client aiming for digital‑first enterprise:  
  - Vision → “Improve customer experience.”  
  - Goal → “Enable real‑time personalization.”  
  - Initiative → “Deploy AI recommendation engine.”  
  - Roadmap → Short‑term API integration, mid‑term microservices adoption, long‑term AI personalization.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How would you apply capability mapping in roadmap creation?  
  A: Identify business capabilities (e.g., customer analytics) and map them to technology enablers (data lakes, BI tools).  
- Q: Why is dependency analysis critical in roadmap planning?  
  A: Ensures logical sequencing (e.g., cloud migration before microservices), preventing delays and resource conflicts.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Keeping vision too abstract without decomposition.  
- Ignoring capability mapping, leading to isolated technical upgrades.  
- Overloading teams by skipping phased planning.  
- Neglecting dependencies, causing bottlenecks.  
- Poor visualization, weakening stakeholder buy‑in.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Blueprint to Building”** — vision is the design, roadmap techniques are the construction steps.  
- Mnemonic: **“VCTDV → Vision, Capability, Time, Dependency, Visualization.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Roadmap = structured plan from vision to execution.  
- Vision decomposition makes abstract goals actionable.  
- Capability mapping links business needs to IT enablers.  
- Time‑phased planning ensures incremental progress.  
- Dependency analysis prevents sequencing errors.  
- Visualization builds stakeholder trust and clarity.
