# Integration with KPIs
## 1. What is it?
- Ensuring every architecture decision and roadmap initiative is directly linked to measurable business outcomes.  
- Core Infosys concept: KPIs act as the bridge between technology and business strategy.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Guarantees alignment of technology investments with strategic goals.  
- Provides measurable success criteria for architecture initiatives.  
- Improves stakeholder communication by translating technical decisions into business terms.  
- Enables continuous monitoring and adjustment for long‑term impact.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: KPI mapping, scorecards, dashboards, accountability.  
- Situational traps: implementing systems without measurable KPIs.  
- Keywords: revenue growth, operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, NPS.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Infosys client aiming to reduce operational costs by 20%:  
  - Business objective → cost reduction.  
  - KPI → cost per transaction.  
  - Architecture decision → cloud migration.  
  - Outcome → reduced infrastructure costs, tracked via KPI dashboards.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How would you integrate KPIs into architecture planning?  
  A: Identify business objectives, define relevant KPIs, map architecture decisions to KPI outcomes, and track via scorecards/dashboards.  
- Q: Why is KPI integration critical in enterprise governance?  
  A: It ensures accountability, builds stakeholder confidence, and enables data‑driven prioritization.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Selecting KPIs without direct linkage to business goals.  
- Over‑focusing on technical metrics instead of business outcomes.  
- Treating KPI integration as one‑time exercise instead of continuous monitoring.  
- Poor communication of KPI impact to stakeholders.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Bridge Model”** — KPIs are the bridge connecting business strategy (north bank) with architecture execution (south bank).  
- Mnemonic: **“IMMS → Identify, Map, Measure, Scorecard.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Integration = architecture tied to KPIs.  
- KPIs = measurable link between goals and outcomes.  
- Examples: revenue growth, efficiency, satisfaction.  
- Use scorecards to align decisions.  
- Monitor dashboards for continuous improvement.  
- Exam trap: ignoring KPI linkage in planning.
