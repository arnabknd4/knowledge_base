# Why Roadmaps Matter in Modern Enterprises
## 1. What is it?
- A roadmap is a strategic planning tool that connects business vision to execution.  
- Core Infosys concept: roadmaps bridge high‑level goals with actionable initiatives.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Prevents misalignment, wasted resources, and missed opportunities.  
- Provides clarity, prioritization, and stakeholder communication.  
- Enables proactive risk management and continuous adaptation to market changes.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: vision translation, prioritization, alignment, adaptability.  
- Situational traps: treating roadmaps as static timelines instead of living documents.  
- Keywords: MoSCoW, Value vs. Effort, prioritization, risk management.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Infosys client aiming for global scalability:  
  - Roadmap phases → Cloud migration → Microservices adoption → AI‑driven personalization.  
  - Outcome → Structured execution, reduced silos, improved customer experience.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How would you use a roadmap to align IT initiatives with business goals?  
  A: Break down strategic vision into phased technology initiatives, prioritize using frameworks, and communicate via visual roadmaps.  
- Q: Why are roadmaps critical in stakeholder communication?  
  A: They provide transparency, show alignment with business outcomes, and secure executive buy‑in.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating roadmaps as fixed timelines instead of adaptive tools.  
- Ignoring prioritization frameworks, leading to resource misallocation.  
- Failing to communicate roadmap impact in business terms.  
- Overlooking dependencies and risks.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Roadmap as GPS”** — vision is the destination, roadmap is the GPS guiding execution.  
- Mnemonic: **“CAPER → Clarity, Alignment, Prioritization, Engagement, Risk.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Roadmap = bridge between vision and execution.  
- Translates goals into actionable steps.  
- Provides clarity and alignment across teams.  
- Prioritizes investments using frameworks.  
- Improves stakeholder communication and trust.  
- Enables risk management and adaptation.

- Consider outside material: Gartner roadmap frameworks, McKinsey prioritization matrices, TOGAF ADM roadmap practices.
