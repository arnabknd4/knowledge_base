# Case Study & Discussion – Domino’s Pizza
## 1. What is it?
- A real‑world example of architecture driving digital transformation and measurable business outcomes.  
- Core Infosys concept: technology alignment with business strategy enables competitive advantage.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Demonstrates how architecture decisions directly impact KPIs like revenue, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.  
- Provides a benchmark for enterprise digital transformation initiatives.  
- Reinforces the role of architects as strategic enablers.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: cloud‑native, microservices, omnichannel, analytics.  
- Situational traps: ignoring customer experience or failing to tie architecture to KPIs.  
- Keywords: scalability, omnichannel, NPS, digital‑first.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Domino’s migrated to cloud‑native platforms for scalability.  
- Adopted microservices for faster feature deployment.  
- Integrated omnichannel APIs (web, mobile, voice assistants).  
- Leveraged real‑time analytics for customer behavior and delivery optimization.  
- Outcome → Digital sales grew from 30% to 70% of revenue in 5 years.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: What architecture decisions were critical to Domino’s success?  
  A: Cloud‑native migration, microservices adoption, omnichannel integration, and analytics platforms.  
- Q: How did these decisions align with KPIs?  
  A: Scalability → revenue growth, route optimization → efficiency, personalization → customer satisfaction.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating digital transformation as only a technology upgrade.  
- Ignoring omnichannel integration and customer experience.  
- Failing to measure architecture impact through KPIs.  
- Underestimating governance and stakeholder engagement.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Pizza Tech Stack”** — base (cloud), toppings (microservices), sauce (analytics), delivery box (omnichannel).  
- Mnemonic: **“CMOD → Cloud, Microservices, Omnichannel, Data.”**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Domino’s pivoted to digital‑first strategy.  
- Cloud‑native + microservices = scalability + agility.  
- Omnichannel APIs = seamless customer experience.  
- Analytics = optimized delivery + personalization.  
- KPIs tracked: revenue growth, efficiency, NPS.  
- Impact: transformed into a “tech company that sells pizza.”
