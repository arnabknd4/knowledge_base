# Business Strategy vs. Technology Strategy
## 1. What is it?
- Business strategy defines long‑term organizational goals and plans to achieve them.  
- Technology strategy outlines how technology supports and enables those business goals.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Ensures technology investments deliver measurable business outcomes.  
- Prevents wasted resources and missed opportunities due to misalignment.  
- Strengthens governance, scalability, and innovation in enterprise contexts.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: alignment, dependency, enablement.  
- Situational traps: treating technology strategy as independent of business needs.  
- Keywords: vision, mission, roadmap, governance, scalability.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Infosys client expanding into new markets:  
  - Business strategy → increase customer retention by 20%.  
  - Technology strategy → implement cloud‑native architecture to scale globally and reduce costs by 30%.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How would you align technology strategy with business goals in a consulting engagement?  
  A: Map business KPIs (growth, efficiency, retention) to technology enablers (cloud, AI, automation).  
- Q: What happens if technology strategy is created in isolation?  
  A: Leads to silos, wasted investments, and inability to support business pivots.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Assuming technology strategy can exist independently.  
- Over‑focusing on emerging tech without business relevance.  
- Ignoring governance and compliance frameworks.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Two gears”** — business strategy is the driver gear, technology strategy is the follower gear.  
- Mnemonic: **“VRT → Vision, Roadmap, Technology”** (business vision → roadmap → enabling tech).

## 8. Revision Summary
- Business strategy = goals, markets, efficiency.  
- Technology strategy = enabler, roadmap, governance.  
- Must align → tech serves business.  
- Misalignment = wasted resources, missed opportunities.  
- Example: cloud adoption to support global scalability.  
- Keywords: vision, mission, roadmap, compliance.

## 9. Outside Exposure
- Consider outside material: TOGAF Business–IT alignment frameworks, Gartner reports on digital strategy integration.
