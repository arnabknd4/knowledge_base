# Why Architecture Alignment Matters in Modern Enterprises
## 1. What is it?
- Ensuring software architecture decisions are directly tied to business strategy.
- Core Infosys concept: technology must deliver measurable business value.

## 2. Why do we need it?
- Prevents siloed investments and wasted resources.
- Drives scalability, agility, efficiency, and innovation.
- Strengthens governance and supports digital transformation.

## 3. Core Exam Focus
- Principles: business–IT alignment, agility, risk reduction.
- Situational traps: confusing technical optimization with strategic alignment.
- Keywords: scalability, adaptability, compliance, digital transformation.

## 4. Real‑World Use Case
- Infosys enterprise project: migrating legacy monoliths to cloud‑native microservices.
- Alignment ensures faster time‑to‑market, reduced costs, and improved customer experience.

## 5. Interview Lens
- Q: How would you align architecture with business KPIs in a client engagement?  
  A: Map architecture decisions (e.g., microservices, event‑driven design) to KPIs like agility, cost efficiency, and customer satisfaction.  
- Q: What risks arise if architecture is misaligned with strategy?  
  A: Increased technical debt, higher costs, slower innovation, and loss of competitive advantage.

## 6. Common Mistakes
- Treating architecture as purely technical, ignoring business outcomes.
- Over‑engineering solutions without mapping to strategic goals.
- Neglecting stakeholder communication in business terms.

## 7. Mental Model
- Analogy: **“Architecture is the bridge”** — connects business vision (north bank) with technology execution (south bank).
- Mnemonic: **“ABC” → Alignment, Business outcomes, Competitive edge.**

## 8. Revision Summary
- Alignment = architecture decisions tied to strategy.
- Drives KPIs: growth, efficiency, customer satisfaction.
- Reduces risk: compliance, resilience, future‑proofing.
- Enables agility: modular, cloud‑native, adaptable.
- Builds trust: architects speak business language.
- Competitive advantage through faster innovation.
