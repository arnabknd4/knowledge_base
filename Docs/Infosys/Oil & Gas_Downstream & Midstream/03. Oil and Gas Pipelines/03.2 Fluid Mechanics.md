## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  Fluid mechanics studies how liquids and gases behave under forces, pressure, and flow conditions.

- **Plain-English explanation (3-4 lines):**  
  In pipelines, fluid mechanics explains how hydrocarbons move, what pressure is needed, and how friction or turbulence affects flow. Engineers use continuity, Bernoulli’s principle, and energy-loss equations to design safe and efficient systems.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  Ensures pipelines are designed to handle flow rates, avoid surges, and minimize energy losses.

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Continuity Equation:** \(Q = A \cdot v\) (flow rate constant).  
  - **Bernoulli’s Principle:** Energy conservation in fluid flow.  
  - **Reynolds Number:** Predicts laminar vs turbulent flow.  
  - **Darcy-Weisbach Equation:** Head loss due to friction.

## 3. 🎯 Exam Focus
- **Likely question types:** Formula application, flow regime identification, energy loss calculation.  
- **Must-memorize facts:**  
  - Continuity: \(Q = A \cdot v\).  
  - Bernoulli: \(P + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho gh = \text{constant}\).  
  - Reynolds: \(Re = \frac{\rho v D}{\mu}\).  
  - Darcy-Weisbach: \(h_f = f \cdot \frac{L}{D} \cdot \frac{v^2}{2g}\).  
- **Keywords that signal this topic in a question:** Flow rate, pressure drop, laminar/turbulent, head loss.  
- **Commonly confused with:** Thermodynamics (focus is energy, not flow).  
- **Elimination tips:** If question mentions velocity profiles or friction factors, it’s fluid mechanics.

## 5. Q&A Bank
### Quick recall
- **Q:** What equation predicts laminar vs turbulent flow?  
  **A:** Reynolds number.  

### Conceptual
- **Q:** Why is Bernoulli’s principle important?  
  **A:** It explains energy conservation in fluid flow.  

### Scenario-based
- **Q:** A pipeline faces high friction losses. Which formula applies?  
  **A:** Darcy-Weisbach equation.  

### Interview-style
- **Q:** "Explain fluid mechanics to a non-technical executive."  
  **A:** It’s the science of how liquids and gases move, helping engineers design pipelines that flow safely and efficiently.

## 6. ✅ Dos and ❌ Don'ts
| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Apply continuity & Bernoulli | Ignore friction losses |
| Check flow regime | Assume all flows are laminar |
| Use hydraulic simulations | Rely only on manual estimates |

- **Common mistakes / misconceptions:**  
  1. Confusing laminar with turbulent flow.  
  2. Ignoring minor losses in fittings/valves.  

- **Red flags in practice:** Uncalculated surges, underestimated head losses.
