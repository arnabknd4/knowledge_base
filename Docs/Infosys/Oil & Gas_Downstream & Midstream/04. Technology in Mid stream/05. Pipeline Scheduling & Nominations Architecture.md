## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  Pipeline integrity and regulatory standards ensure pipelines are designed, operated, and retired safely under strict codes and monitoring systems.

- **Plain-English explanation (3-4 lines):**  
  Integrity management involves continuous monitoring, inspection, and maintenance to prevent leaks or failures. Regulatory standards enforce compliance with codes (ASME, API, PHMSA). Decommissioning ensures pipelines are retired responsibly, minimizing environmental and social risks.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  Protects people, environment, and assets while ensuring legal compliance and operational reliability.

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Code Compliance:** Adhering to ASME, API, PHMSA standards.  
  - **Safety Monitoring:** Inline inspection, SCADA, leak detection.  
  - **Decommissioning:** Safe retirement, environmental restoration.

## 2. Mental Model
- **Analogy:** "Pipeline integrity is like regular health check-ups—monitoring, compliance, and safe retirement keep the system healthy throughout its life."  

- **Text Diagram / flow:**  
  Code Compliance → Safety Monitoring → Integrity Management → Decommissioning  

- **The one thing to remember:**  
  Integrity = continuous monitoring + compliance + safe retirement.

## 3. 🎯 Exam Focus
- **Likely question types:** Define integrity management, explain decommissioning, identify standards.  
- **Must-memorize facts:**  
  - ASME B31.4/B31.8 = pipeline codes.  
  - API 1160 = integrity management.  
  - PHMSA = US pipeline safety regulator.  
  - Decommissioning = safe retirement + restoration.  
- **Keywords that signal this topic in a question:** Integrity, compliance, PHMSA, decommissioning.  
- **Commonly confused with:** EPC execution (construction focus).  
- **Elimination tips:** If question mentions retirement or monitoring, it’s integrity/regulatory.
