## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  Digital twins are virtual replicas of physical assets, integrated with IoT and SCADA data, enabling predictive maintenance and real-time optimization.

- **Plain-English explanation (3-4 lines):**  
  A digital twin mirrors pipeline or station operations using live sensor data. IoT networks feed continuous flow/pressure readings, while SCADA systems integrate control and monitoring. Predictive analytics forecast failures, allowing proactive maintenance before breakdowns occur.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  Reduces downtime, improves safety, and optimizes asset lifecycle costs.

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Digital Twin:** Virtual model of a physical asset.  
  - **IoT Sensor Networks:** Real-time data collection (pressure, flow, vibration).  
  - **SCADA Integration:** Supervisory control and data acquisition systems.  
  - **Predictive Maintenance:** AI-driven failure forecasting.

- **Likely question types:** Define digital twin, explain predictive maintenance, scenario-based IoT/SCADA integration.  
- **Must-memorize facts:**

  - SCADA = control + monitoring backbone.  
  - Digital twin = predictive simulation.  
- **Keywords that signal this topic in a question:** Digital twin, predictive, IoT, SCADA.
