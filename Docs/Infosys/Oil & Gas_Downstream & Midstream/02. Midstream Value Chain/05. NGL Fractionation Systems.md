## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  NGL fractionation systems separate mixed natural gas liquids into individual components like ethane, propane, butane, and natural gasoline.

- **Plain-English explanation (3-4 lines):**  
  Natural gas streams often contain a mix of hydrocarbons called NGLs. Fractionation plants use distillation towers to separate these into pure products. Each fraction has unique industrial uses—ethane for petrochemicals, propane for heating, butane for blending, and natural gasoline for fuel. This step ensures value-added downstream applications.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  Converts mixed NGL streams into usable, market-ready products with distinct commercial value.

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Ethane:** Feedstock for ethylene production.  
  - **Propane:** Heating fuel, petrochemical feedstock.  
  - **Butane:** Gasoline blending, LPG.  
  - **Natural Gasoline (Pentanes+):** Blending component, petrochemical feedstock.  

- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. Feed mixed NGL stream into fractionation towers.  
  2. Separate by boiling point differences.  
  3. Collect individual products (ethane, propane, butane, gasoline).  
  4. Store and transport for downstream use.  

- **Where it sits in the AI stack:** Data (composition) → Model (distillation optimization) → Application (fractionation ops) → Governance (safety standards).  

- **Related topics:** Gas Pre-Processing, Petrochemical Integration

## 2. Mental Model
- **Analogy:** "Fractionation is like sorting a bag of mixed candies into separate jars—each candy type has its own use and value."  

- **Text Diagram / flow:**  
  Mixed NGLs → Fractionation Towers → Ethane / Propane / Butane / Natural Gasoline  

- **The one thing to remember:**  
  Fractionation = separation of NGLs into distinct, valuable products.  

- **Compare and contrast:**

| | NGL Fractionation | Refining Distillation |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Separate NGLs | Separate crude oil fractions |
| Focus | Ethane, propane, butane, gasoline | Gasoline, diesel, kerosene, etc. |
| Key difference | Midstream process | Downstream process |

## 3. 🎯 Exam Focus
- **Likely question types:** Identify NGL products, scenario-based separation, compare fractionation vs refining.  
- **Must-memorize facts:**  
  - Ethane = petrochemical feedstock.  
  - Propane = heating + petrochemicals.  
  - Butane = LPG + blending.  
  - Natural gasoline = blending + feedstock.  
- **Keywords that signal this topic in a question:** Fractionation, NGL, ethane, propane, butane, gasoline.  
- **Commonly confused with:** Refining distillation (focus is crude oil, not NGLs).  
- **Elimination tips:** If question mentions ethane/propane separation, it’s NGL fractionation.  
- **Frameworks / standards link:** **API 2510** (LPG storage), **ASME Section VIII** (pressure vessels).
