## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  Storage tank architecture refers to the design and types of tanks used to store hydrocarbons safely and efficiently in midstream operations.

- **Plain-English explanation (3-4 lines):**  
  Hydrocarbons must be stored before refining or distribution. Different tank designs—fixed roof, floating roof, bullet tanks, and spherical containers—are used depending on the product type, pressure, and safety requirements. Proper architecture ensures safety, minimizes losses, and balances supply-demand.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  Prevents product loss, ensures safety, and provides flexibility in handling different hydrocarbon types.

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Fixed Roof Tanks:** For low-volatility liquids.  
  - **Floating Roof Tanks:** Reduce vapor losses for volatile liquids.  
  - **Bullet Tanks:** Cylindrical, high-pressure storage for LPG/NGL.  
  - **Spherical Containers:** High-pressure, uniform stress distribution for LNG/LPG.  

- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. Select tank type based on hydrocarbon properties.  
  2. Store product under controlled conditions.  
  3. Monitor safety and emissions.  
  4. Integrate with pipelines and distribution systems.  

- **Where it sits in the AI stack:** Data (tank levels, pressure) → Model (inventory optimization) → Application (supply chain ops) → Governance (safety standards).  

- **Related topics:** Hydrocarbon Gathering, Gas Pre-Processing

## 3. 🎯 Exam Focus
- **Likely question types:** Identify tank types, match product to tank, scenario-based safety.  
- **Must-memorize facts:**  
  - Fixed roof = low-volatility liquids.  
  - Floating roof = volatile liquids, vapor control.  
  - Bullet tanks = LPG/NGL, high pressure.  
  - Spherical = LNG/LPG, uniform stress.  
- **Keywords that signal this topic in a question:** Fixed roof, floating roof, bullet, spherical.  
- **Commonly confused with:** Refinery unit vessels (these are storage, not processing).  
- **Elimination tips:** If question mentions vapor control, it’s floating roof.  
- **Frameworks / standards link:** **API 650** (tank design), **API 620** (low-pressure tanks), **ASME Section VIII** (pressure vessels).
