## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:** ISO/IEC 42001 is the first international standard for AI management systems, providing requirements for responsible AI governance.
- **Plain-English explanation (3–4 lines):**  
  It’s like ISO 9001 for AI. Organizations can certify against ISO/IEC 42001 to show they manage AI responsibly. It sets requirements for policies, risk management, transparency, accountability, and continual improvement in AI systems.
- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  - Provides a certifiable global standard for AI governance  
  - Aligns AI practices with safety, ethics, and compliance  
  - Builds trust with regulators, customers, and stakeholders  
- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Management system standard (MSS):** Framework for organizational processes (like ISO 9001, 27001)  
  - **Certification:** Independent audit to prove compliance  
  - **Risk-based approach:** Identify, assess, and mitigate AI risks  
  - **Continuous improvement:** Ongoing monitoring and updates  
- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. Establish AI governance policies.  
  2. Identify and assess AI risks.  
  3. Implement controls for safety, fairness, transparency.  
  4. Monitor and audit AI systems.  
  5. Continually improve processes.  
- **Where it sits in the AI stack:** Organizational governance and compliance layer.  
- **Related topics:** [[NIST AI RMF]], [[EU AI Act]], [[Alignment & AI Safety]]

## 5. Q&A Bank
- **Q:** What is ISO/IEC 42001?  
  **A:** The first international certifiable standard for AI management systems.  

- **Q:** How does it differ from NIST AI RMF?  
  **A:** RMF = voluntary framework; ISO/IEC 42001 = certifiable governance standard.  

- **Q:** How does it differ from the EU AI Act?  
  **A:** EU AI Act = binding regulation; ISO/IEC 42001 = certifiable standard.  

- **Q:** What cycle does ISO/IEC 42001 follow?  
  **A:** Plan–Do–Check–Act (continuous improvement).
