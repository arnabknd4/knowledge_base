## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:** The NIST AI RMF is a framework developed by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology to help organizations manage risks associated with AI systems.
- **Plain-English explanation (3–4 lines):**  
  It provides guidelines for designing, developing, deploying, and using AI responsibly. The framework emphasizes trustworthiness, safety, fairness, and accountability. It’s voluntary but widely adopted as a global reference for AI risk management.
- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  - Helps organizations identify and mitigate AI risks  
  - Provides a common language for AI governance  
  - Builds trust in AI systems across industries  
- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Trustworthiness:** Reliability, safety, fairness, transparency  
  - **Risk categories:** Technical, societal, ethical, legal  
  - **Governance functions:** Map, Measure, Manage, Govern  
- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. **Map** AI risks and contexts.  
  2. **Measure** risks using metrics and benchmarks.  
  3. **Manage** risks with mitigation strategies.  
  4. **Govern** AI systems with oversight and accountability.  
- **Where it sits in the AI stack:** Governance and compliance layer.

## 5. Q&A Bank
- **Q:** What is the NIST AI RMF?  
  **A:** A voluntary framework for managing risks in AI systems, focusing on trustworthiness.  

- **Q:** What are its four core functions?  
  **A:** Map, Measure, Manage, Govern.  

- **Q:** How does it differ from ISO AI standards?  
  **A:** RMF = voluntary risk framework; ISO = formal certification standards.  

- **Q:** Why is RMF important?  
  **A:** It builds trust in AI by ensuring safety, fairness, and accountability.
