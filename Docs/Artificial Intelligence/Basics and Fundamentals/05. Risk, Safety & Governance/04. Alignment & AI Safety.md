## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:** Alignment ensures AI systems act according to human values and intentions; AI safety ensures they do so reliably without harm.
- **Plain-English explanation (3–4 lines):**  
  Alignment is about making sure AI does what humans want — not just what is technically possible. Safety is about preventing harmful, biased, or unintended outcomes. Together, they form the foundation for trustworthy AI, covering both ethical goals and technical reliability.
- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  - Prevents AI from producing unsafe or harmful outputs  
  - Ensures AI reflects human values and societal norms  
  - Builds trust in AI adoption across industries  
- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Value alignment:** AI outputs match human ethical standards  
  - **Robustness:** AI performs reliably under varied conditions  
  - **Fairness:** Avoids bias and discrimination  
  - **Transparency:** Clear explanations of AI decisions  
  - **Guardrails:** Technical and policy constraints to prevent misuse  
- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. Define human values and safety requirements.  
  2. Train models with alignment techniques (e.g., RLHF, constitutional AI).  
  3. Evaluate with benchmarks and human oversight.  
  4. Deploy with monitoring and guardrails.  
- **Where it sits in the AI stack:** Governance and deployment layer.  
- **Related topics:** [[RLHF]], [[Human-in-the-Loop]], [[Evaluation (Evals)]], [[Benchmarks]]

## 2. Mental Model
- **Analogy:** Think of AI as a powerful car engine. Alignment is the steering wheel (direction according to human values), and safety is the brakes (preventing harm). Both are needed for a safe journey.  
- **Diagram / flow:**
    - Human values → Alignment methods → AI outputs → Safety checks → Deployment
- **The one thing to remember:** Alignment = values; Safety = reliability. Both are inseparable.  
- **Compare and contrast:**

| | Alignment | Safety |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Human values, ethics | Reliability, harm prevention |
| Techniques | RLHF, constitutional AI | Guardrails, monitoring, robustness |
| Outcome | Outputs match human intent | Outputs avoid harm |

## 3. 🎯 Exam Focus
- **Likely question types:** Definition / Scenario (unsafe AI output) / Compare alignment vs safety  
- **Must-memorize facts:**  
  - [ ] Alignment = AI reflects human values  
  - [ ] Safety = AI avoids harm and bias  
  - [ ] Techniques: RLHF, constitutional AI, guardrails  
  - [ ] HITL + monitoring are essential for deployment  
- **Keywords:** "alignment," "AI safety," "RLHF," "guardrails," "robustness"  
- **Commonly confused with:**  
  - Alignment vs accuracy (values vs correctness)  
  - Safety vs security (harm prevention vs protection from attacks)  
- **Elimination tips:**  
  - Reject “alignment = accuracy only.”  
  - Reject “safety = cybersecurity only.”
