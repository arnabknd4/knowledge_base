## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:** Jailbreaking is the act of deliberately bypassing an AI system’s safety rules or restrictions to force it into unsafe or unintended behavior.
- **Plain-English explanation (3–4 lines):**  
  Jailbreaks are attempts by users to trick an AI into ignoring its guardrails. For example, a user might disguise a harmful request as a roleplay or use indirect wording to bypass filters. Unlike prompt injection (malicious hidden instructions), jailbreaks are intentional attempts to override safety constraints.
- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  - Exploits the AI’s reliance on text instructions  
  - Allows users to access restricted or unsafe outputs  
  - Poses risks for safety, compliance, and trust  
- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Roleplay jailbreak:** Pretending the AI is a character with no rules  
  - **Indirect jailbreak:** Asking in roundabout ways to bypass filters  
  - **Chain-of-thought exposure:** Forcing AI to reveal hidden reasoning or instructions  
  - **Guardrails:** Safety mechanisms designed to block jailbreak attempts  
- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. User crafts input designed to bypass safety rules.  
  2. AI interprets input as valid instructions.  
  3. AI produces restricted or unsafe output.  
  4. Guardrails or HITL oversight needed to stop misuse.  
- **Where it sits in the AI stack:** Security risk at inference layer.  
- **Related topics:** [[Prompt Injection]], [[Red Teaming]], [[Alignment & AI Safety]]

## 2. Mental Model
- **Analogy:** Jailbreaking is like tricking a locked phone into running unauthorized apps. The AI has safety locks, but jailbreak attempts try to break those locks with clever wording.  
- **Diagram / flow:**
    - User input → Jailbreak attempt → AI bypasses guardrails → Unsafe output
- **The one thing to remember:** Jailbreaks = intentional attempts to bypass AI safety rules.  
- **Compare and contrast:**

| | Prompt Injection | Jailbreak |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Malicious hidden instructions | User deliberately bypassing rules |
| Intent | Trick AI via external data | Override safety constraints |
| Risk | Data leaks, tool misuse | Unsafe or restricted outputs |

## 5. Q&A Bank
- **Q:** What is a jailbreak in AI?  
  **A:** An attempt to bypass AI safety rules to force unsafe or restricted outputs.  

- **Q:** How does jailbreak differ from prompt injection?  
  **A:** Jailbreak = intentional bypass; injection = hidden malicious instructions.  

- **Q:** Name one common jailbreak technique.  
  **A:** Roleplay jailbreak (pretending AI is a character with no rules).  

- **Q:** How can jailbreaks be mitigated?  
  **A:** Guardrails, monitoring, and human oversight.
