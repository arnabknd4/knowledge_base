## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  Escalation and ambiguity resolution are human handoff patterns where AI defers to humans when risk thresholds are exceeded or outputs are uncertain.

- **Plain-English explanation (3–4 lines):**  
  Escalation occurs when an AI system encounters high‑risk or sensitive cases that require human judgment.  
  Ambiguity resolution happens when the AI produces conflicting or unclear outputs, prompting human intervention.  
  Both patterns ensure safety, accountability, and trust in AI workflows.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  Prevents unchecked AI decisions, clarifies uncertainty, and enforces governance.

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Escalation:** Human review triggered by risk.  
  - **Ambiguity resolution:** Human clarifies uncertain outputs.  
  - **Human‑in‑the‑loop (HITL):** Oversight integrated into workflows.

- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. AI detects risk or uncertainty.  
  2. Flags case for human review.  
  3. Human provides judgment or clarification.  
  4. AI resumes workflow with updated input.

- **Where it sits in the AI stack:**  
  Governance & application layer.

- **Related topics:** Risk thresholds, fallback strategies.

## 2. Mental Model
- **Analogy:**  
  Escalation = customer service agent escalating to a manager.  
  Ambiguity resolution = asking a supervisor to clarify unclear instructions.

- **Text Diagram / flow:**  
  AI → Detects risk/uncertainty → Human review → Clarification/decision → AI continues

- **The one thing to remember:**  
  Escalation = risk; Ambiguity resolution = uncertainty.

- **Compare and contrast:**

| | Escalation | Ambiguity Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Handle high‑risk cases | Resolve uncertainty |
| Trigger | Risk threshold exceeded | Conflicting/unclear outputs |
| Human role | Final decision‑maker | Clarifier of ambiguity |

## 6. ✅ Dos and ❌ Don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Define clear thresholds | Let AI handle critical risk unchecked |
| Document human decisions | Ignore ambiguity logs |
| Train staff for oversight | Assume humans always know context |

- **Common mistakes / misconceptions:**  
  1. Thinking escalation = ambiguity resolution (they’re distinct).  
  2. Assuming handoff slows workflows without considering risk mitigation.

- **Red flags in practice:**  
  No documented thresholds for escalation.
