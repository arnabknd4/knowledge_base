## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  Confidence scoring assigns probability levels to AI outputs, while human review workflows ensure oversight when confidence is low or risk is high.

- **Plain-English explanation (3–4 lines):**  
  Confidence scores quantify how certain the model is about its output.  
  Low scores trigger human review workflows, where humans validate or override the AI’s decision.  
  This balance ensures efficiency while maintaining safety and accountability.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  Prevents blind trust in AI, reduces risk of errors, and enforces governance in critical workflows.

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Confidence score:** Probability estimate of correctness.  
  - **Thresholds:** Cut‑offs for when human review is required.  
  - **Human‑in‑the‑loop (HITL):** Oversight mechanism.  
  - **Override:** Human decision replaces AI output.

- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. AI generates output + confidence score.  
  2. Compare score against threshold.  
  3. If below threshold → escalate to human review.  
  4. Human validates, corrects, or overrides.  
  5. AI resumes workflow with updated input.

- **Where it sits in the AI stack:**  
  Governance → oversight → assurance.

- **Related topics:** Escalation, ambiguity resolution, error propagation.

## 2. Mental Model
- **Analogy:**  
  “Confidence scoring is like a weather forecast — if the probability of rain is low, you trust the forecast; if it’s uncertain, you double‑check before planning.”

- **Text Diagram / flow:**  
  Output + Confidence Score → Threshold Check → Human Review (if low) → Final Decision

- **The one thing to remember:**  
  Confidence scoring = probability; human review = safety net.

- **Compare and contrast:**

| | Confidence Scoring | Human Review Workflow |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Quantify certainty | Validate uncertain outputs |
| Trigger | Probability estimate | Low confidence / high risk |
| Human role | Threshold setting | Oversight + override |

## 3. 🎯 Exam Focus
- **Likely question types:**  
  Definition, scenario (low confidence handling), compare/contrast.

- **Must-memorize facts:**  
  - Confidence scoring = probability estimate.  
  - Human review = oversight when confidence is low.  
  - Thresholds define when review is triggered.

- **Keywords that signal this topic in a question:**  
  “confidence score,” “human review,” “threshold.”

- **Commonly confused with:**  
  Explicit criteria (rule‑based thresholds, not probability).  

- **Elimination tips:**  
  If probability mentioned → confidence scoring.  
  If rule‑based thresholds → explicit criteria.

- **Frameworks / standards link:**  
  NIST AI RMF (risk thresholds), EU AI Act (human oversight).

## 6. ✅ Dos and ❌ Don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Define clear thresholds | Leave confidence unmonitored |
| Document human overrides | Skip review logs |
| Train reviewers | Assume humans always know context |

- **Common mistakes / misconceptions:**  
  1. Confusing confidence scoring with explicit criteria.  
  2. Assuming high confidence = always correct.

- **Red flags in practice:**  
  No thresholds, no review logs, blind trust in AI.
