## 1. Learn It (Core Understanding)
- **One-line definition:**  
  Human handoff patterns define when and how AI systems transfer control to humans for oversight, decision-making, or resolution.

- **Plain-English explanation (3–4 lines):**  
  Escalation occurs when an AI encounters a high-risk, sensitive, or complex case that requires human judgment. Ambiguity resolution happens when the AI is uncertain or produces conflicting outputs, prompting human intervention to clarify. These patterns ensure safety, accountability, and trust in AI workflows.

- **Why it exists / problem it solves:**  
  - Prevents AI from making unchecked critical decisions  
  - Provides oversight in ambiguous or high-risk contexts  
  - Builds user trust by ensuring humans remain in the loop  

- **Key terms to know:**  
  - **Escalation:** AI defers to human when risk threshold is exceeded  
  - **Ambiguity resolution:** Human clarifies uncertain/conflicting outputs  
  - **Human-in-the-loop (HITL):** Humans integrated into AI workflows  

- **How it works (simple steps):**  
  1. AI detects risk or uncertainty  
  2. Flags case for human review  
  3. Human provides judgment or clarification  
  4. AI resumes workflow with updated input  

- **Where it sits in the AI stack:** Governance & application layer  
- **Related topics:** [[Risk thresholds]], [[Human-in-the-loop]], [[AI assurance]]

## 2. Mental Model
- **Analogy:**  
  Escalation = customer service agent escalating to a manager.  
  Ambiguity resolution = asking a supervisor to clarify unclear instructions.  

- **Diagram / flow:**
    - AI → Detects risk/uncertainty → Human review → Clarification/decision → AI continues

- **The one thing to remember:**  
  Escalation = risk; Ambiguity resolution = uncertainty.  

- **Compare and contrast:**

| | Escalation | Ambiguity Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Handle high-risk cases | Resolve uncertainty |
| Trigger | Risk threshold exceeded | Conflicting/unclear outputs |
| Human role | Final decision-maker | Clarifier of ambiguity |

## 6. ✅ Dos and ❌ Don’ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don’t |
|---|---|
| Define clear thresholds for handoff | Let AI handle critical risk unchecked |
| Document human decisions | Ignore ambiguity logs |
| Train staff for oversight | Assume humans will always know context |

- **Common mistakes / misconceptions:**  
  1. Thinking escalation = ambiguity resolution (they’re distinct).  
  2. Assuming handoff slows workflows without considering risk mitigation.  

- **Red flags in practice:** No documented thresholds for escalation.
