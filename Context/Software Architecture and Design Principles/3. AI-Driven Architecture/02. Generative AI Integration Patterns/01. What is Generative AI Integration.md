# 01. What is Generative AI Integration
Generative AI refers to AI models that create new content—text, images, audio, code, or synthetic data—based on patterns learned from existing data. Integrating these capabilities into enterprise systems requires architectural patterns that ensure:

Scalability for large workloads.
Security and compliance for sensitive data.
Performance optimization for real-time use cases.
Business alignment to deliver measurable value.
Why Integration Patterns Are Critical
Standardization: Provides repeatable, proven methods for connecting GenAI to enterprise workflows.
Risk Mitigation: Ensures compliance, security, and ethical AI use.
Cost Efficiency: Optimizes infrastructure and reduces duplication.
Business Alignment: Links AI capabilities to measurable outcomes.
