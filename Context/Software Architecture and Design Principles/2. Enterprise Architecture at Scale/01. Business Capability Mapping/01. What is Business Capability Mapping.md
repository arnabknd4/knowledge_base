# What is Business Capability Mapping?

Business Capability Mapping is a strategic planning technique that provides a high-level view of what an organization needs to be able to do to achieve its business objectives. It focuses on capabilities—the fundamental abilities or competencies of the business—rather than processes, organizational structures, or technology.

A business capability answers the question:
“What does the business need to do to deliver value?”
For example: Customer Relationship Management, Order Fulfillment, Risk Management.

Key Characteristics of Business Capabilities

Stable over time: Capabilities remain relatively constant even when processes or technologies change.
Independent of organizational structure: They describe what the business does, not who does it.
Technology-agnostic: They are not tied to specific systems or tools.
Purpose of Business Capability Mapping

Strategic Alignment: Connects business strategy to execution by identifying which capabilities are critical for achieving goals.
Investment Prioritization: Helps decide where to allocate resources and technology investments.
Transformation Planning: Provides a roadmap for improving or building capabilities to support growth or change.
Communication Tool: Offers a clear, visual representation of business needs for executives and stakeholders.
How It Works

Identify Capabilities: Break down the organization into a set of capabilities (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Logistics).
Organize into Layers:
Level 1: High-level domains (e.g., Customer Management).
Level 2: Sub-capabilities (e.g., Customer Analytics, Customer Support).
Assess Maturity: Evaluate current performance vs. desired state.
Map to Strategy and Technology: Link capabilities to business objectives and enabling systems.
Prioritize Gaps: Focus on capabilities that need improvement to deliver strategic outcomes.
Why It’s Important

Provides a business-centric view rather than a technology-centric view.
Enables better decision-making for IT investments and transformation programs.
Helps avoid duplication and identify gaps in capabilities.
