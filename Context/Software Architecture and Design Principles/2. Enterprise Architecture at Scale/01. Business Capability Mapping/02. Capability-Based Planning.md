# Capability-Based Planning

Capability-Based Planning (CBP) is a strategic approach to planning and prioritizing initiatives based on the capabilities an organization needs to achieve its business objectives. Instead of starting with projects, technologies, or organizational structures, CBP begins by asking:

“What capabilities do we need to deliver our strategy?”

A capability is a high-level ability of the business to perform a function or deliver value (e.g., Customer Analytics, Order Fulfillment, Risk Management). CBP ensures that every investment—whether in technology, process improvement, or people—directly supports building or enhancing these capabilities.

Why is CBP Important?

Aligns IT and business strategy: Ensures technology investments are driven by business needs, not trends.
Improves prioritization: Focuses resources on the most critical capabilities for achieving strategic goals.
Supports agility: Capabilities are stable over time, even when processes or technologies change, making planning more resilient.
Enables transformation: Provides a roadmap for evolving capabilities to meet future demands.
Core Principles of CBP:

Start with Strategy
Understand the organization’s vision, goals, and objectives. Identify which capabilities are essential to deliver these outcomes.
Identify and Define Capabilities
Break down the business into a set of capabilities (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Logistics). Each capability should be:
Stable (not tied to a specific process or system)
Business-focused (what the business does, not how)
Assess Capability Maturity
Evaluate the current state of each capability against desired performance. Use maturity models to identify gaps.
Prioritize Gaps
Focus on capabilities that:
Have the highest impact on strategic objectives
Are currently weak or missing
Enable competitive advantage
Plan Initiatives
Develop projects and programs to build or enhance these capabilities. Link initiatives to measurable outcomes.
How CBP Works in Practice:

Example Scenario:
A retail company wants to improve customer experience.
Strategic Goal: Enhance personalization and reduce delivery time.
Required Capabilities:
Customer Analytics (to understand buying behavior)
Order Fulfillment (to speed up delivery)
Initiatives:
Invest in advanced analytics tools
Upgrade warehouse management systems
Benefits of CBP:

Transparency: Stakeholders see how investments support business goals.
Efficiency: Avoids redundant projects and technology spend.
Flexibility: Adapts to changing market conditions without losing focus.
