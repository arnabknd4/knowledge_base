# Case Study & Discussion
Case Study: Domino’s Pizza – Architecture Driving Business Transformation

Background:
Domino’s Pizza faced declining sales and customer dissatisfaction in the early 2010s. The company realized that its business strategy needed to focus on digital transformation to improve customer experience and operational efficiency.

Business Challenge
Slow order processing and delivery times.
Poor customer engagement.
Competitors leveraging digital platforms for faster service.

Strategic Goal
Become a digital-first pizza company by enabling fast, convenient, and personalized ordering experiences.
Improve customer satisfaction and increase revenue through technology-driven innovation.

Architecture Decisions

Cloud-Native Architecture:
Migrated to a scalable cloud platform to handle peak demand and ensure high availability.

Microservices-Based Design:
Broke down monolithic systems into microservices for flexibility and faster feature deployment.

Omnichannel Integration:
Built APIs to integrate web, mobile apps, voice assistants, and smart devices for seamless ordering.
Data Analytics Platform:
Implemented real-time analytics to track customer behavior and optimize delivery routes.
KPIs Tracked

Revenue Growth:
Increased digital sales from 30% to over 70% of total revenue within 5 years.

Operational Efficiency:
Reduced average delivery time by 20% through route optimization.

Customer Satisfaction:
Improved Net Promoter Score (NPS) significantly by offering personalized experiences and faster service.
Impact

Domino’s transformed from a traditional pizza chain into a technology-driven company, often referred to as a “tech company that sells pizza.”
Digital channels became the primary source of revenue, driving double-digit growth year over year.
Discussion Questions

What architecture decisions were critical to Domino’s success?

How did these decisions align with business KPIs?
