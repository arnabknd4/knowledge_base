## PART 1: AUDIT RESULT AND HOW THIS FILE FITS

Purpose: the master, AI and leadership syllabuses cover technology, AI and people leadership very well. They cover enterprise-level thinking only thinly (15.07 TOGAF is the single EA topic). This file fills that gap and then closes the planning. After this file, no more syllabus files (see Part 9).

What is already covered well (do not duplicate):

- Technology and cloud depth: master D01 to D17, languages and AWS D18 to D24.
- AI engineering and AI governance: ai-master-syllabus D25 to D38.
- People, delivery, stakeholder and architecture-practice leadership: leadership-master-syllabus (renumbered D50 to D57, see Part 7).

What was missing for an Enterprise or Principal Architect path:

- EA practice beyond one TOGAF topic: frameworks compared, business architecture, ArchiMate and tooling, application portfolio management, the EA office itself.
- Enterprise data architecture: master data, data platforms, governance, catalog and lineage. The master has only an optional 4.10 and the AI file covers data only for AI.
- Enterprise integration and packaged applications: ESB, iPaaS, API platform strategy, ERP, CRM and SaaS strategy, low-code. Enterprises run on SAP, Salesforce and ServiceNow, not only custom .NET.
- Enterprise security, identity, risk and resilience: SABSA, NIST CSF, enterprise IAM, GRC, RBI and SEBI-type regulation, business continuity. The master covers developer and cloud security, not enterprise security architecture.
- Infrastructure and operations at enterprise scale: data centre and hybrid, enterprise network, ITSM, cloud operating model.
- Strategy and business: strategy frameworks, Wardley mapping, systems thinking, digital transformation, IT financial management, sourcing and GCC models, mergers and acquisitions.
- Industry and domain knowledge: an EA who does not know the business domain is not trusted. Pick two industries.
- Horizon scanning and emerging technology, sustainability.
- Executive presence, professional standing (IASA, Open CA) and the EA interview.
- A practice vehicle: an enterprise capstone (48.01) and case drills (48.02), the same way 17.02 and 38.02 turn notes into skill.

The EA mindset shift (read once a quarter):

- From a correct design to a decision that fits the business for 3 to 5 years.
- From one system to a portfolio of hundreds of systems, most of them bought, not built.
- From technology choices to business capabilities, cost, risk and regulation.
- From one best answer to options, trade-offs and reversibility.
- From authority to influence: you rarely own a team, you own the consistency of decisions.
- From builder to curator: remove, merge and retire as much as you introduce.
- From today to transition states: nothing changes in one step.
