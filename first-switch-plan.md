# First-switch plan (9 months, from Oct 2026)

Goal: first switch to a modern .NET + Azure role at a GCC, consulting or product firm, Kolkata or remote India, target 20-26 LPA CTC (fixed 17-23). Architect roles at 30+ come on the second step.
Budget: 10-15 hours a week. 30 notes plus AZ-104. No new syllabus files and no extra certificates until the first switch is done.
Tier: A = full note (~700 words) plus lab or exercise. B = short note (~300 words).

## Gates
- End of month 3: resume live, ported sample working. If zero recruiter calls by end of month 4, fix resume keywords and apply wider.
- End of month 6: AZ-104 passed. If not, retake in month 7 and do not add anything new.
- Month 9: if no offer at 20 LPA or more, review the plan: add consulting-firm applications, or reconsider the delivery-manager track.

## Notice period
90 days means an offer in month 5 joins in month 8 at best. Start applying by month 3. Ask for a buyout or a shorter notice at offer stage.

## Rules
- Compare offers on fixed pay. Variable is not guaranteed.
- Verify the exam choice on Microsoft Learn before booking.
- After the first switch: AZ-305, then the solution architect route (solution-master-syllabus Part 5).
